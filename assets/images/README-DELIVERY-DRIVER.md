# Delivery Driver Image

Please save your orange delivery driver image (motorcycle with driver) as:

**delivery-driver.png**

in this folder: `terango/assets/images/`

The image should:

- Be a PNG with transparent background
- Feature the orange motorcycle and driver
- Be approximately 300x300px or larger for best quality
- Have the driver and bike facing right for motion effect

The PromoBanner component will automatically display this image with:

- Floating animation
- Glow effects
- Speed lines for dynamic motion
